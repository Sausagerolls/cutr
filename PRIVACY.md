# Cutr Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 October 2026_

Cutr does not collect, transmit or sell any personal data.

- **Your cut lists** are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also stored in your private iCloud database so they sync between your devices. This data is only accessible to you through your Apple Account; the developer cannot see it.
- **No analytics, advertising or tracking** SDKs are included.
- **No account** is required.
- **Exported PDFs** are only shared where you choose to send them.

Deleting the app removes the data from that device. To remove synced data, delete your cut lists in the app or remove Cutr's data under Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Sausagerolls/cutr/issues.
